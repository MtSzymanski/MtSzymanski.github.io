Post 1 - 16/08/2026

I’m in Dresden as part of the 6-week Dive Into Research programme organised by Ben Hollering, Aida Maraj and Irem Portakal [also Kathleen Kulb on the administrative side!]. As part of this six weeks I will try to write a weekly blog post at the end of each week summarising what I got up to.

As I’m writing this, I have just finished my first week of the programme and I’ve had the privilege to meet and work with some incredible people. The first week has mainly focused on lectures and workshop sessions designed to bring everyone to a similar level before projects are assigned and the main research task begins. The material covered was on algebraic geometry and its computational applications to problems in statistics and game theory.

My project will be on studying Level-2 CFN Phylogenetic Networks and trying to answer questions surrounding the parameterization and identifiability of these models; I will be working together with Jakub Mazur, Francesco Nowell, as well as Ben Hollering on this. In many ways this serves as a continuation of my adventure studying the geometry of phylogenetic models which began with my work on them together with Dimitra Kosta (cf. Phylogenetic Algebraic Geometry and Matroid Applications in Phylogenetics Algebraic Geometry).

During the weekend I’ve went to the Dresden Canaletto festival (lot’s of live music and surprisingly Hungarian lángos) as well as on a hike with other DIR participants to Saxon-Switzerland. The latter was a good introduction to the German pastime of hiking and I’ll try later to upload some photos of the mesmerising views of the Malerweg.

Tomorrow, arguably, the real work of the research project begins. There’s a lot of reading ahead that needs to be done, and still lots more to learn about the syntax and functionalities of Macaulay2 and Oscar, but having said that I’m looking forward to the week ahead. Theirs is nothing more to say other than: “let’s calculate!”
