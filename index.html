I'm a recent mathematics masters graduate from the University of Edinburgh, currently looking to apply to a PhD program.

My research interests include noncommutative ring theory, representation theory, algebraic geometry, algebraic topology, category theory, mathematical logic with emphasis on model theory, and the interactions between all those fields. Additionally, I am interested in geometrical and algebraic problems arising from mathematical physics and biology.

On this website you can find out about my the academic credentials in the CV and Papers sections, as well as a little more about my hobbies and interests in the Misc. and Blog sections.

Please don't hesitate to get in touch if you have any questions or want to talk maths!
